## System-Level Feedback

- **Verification-command phrase-matching is brittle against the exact double-occurrence trap it's meant to catch.** T-MIN-015's verification command greps for `arie batch in flight|reconciliation deferred|batch in flight \(T-MIN-011` and `left to that batch to offer` — but these patterns only cover the claims-table row and §3 prose sentence. In this task there was a *third* location carrying stale deferral language: each of the three low-block files' §5 "Verification a reviewer must perform" checklist had its own sentence describing the arie edge as still in-flight (worst case, Ruler IV: "the *arie* deferral is recorded (T-MIN-011 in flight)"). None of these tripped the verification command because the exact phrase orderings didn't match the regex, even though the underlying staleness was the same category of bug. Recommend PMs writing verification commands for "reconcile deferred X" tasks grep more broadly (e.g. just `in.flight` or `T-MIN-011` combined with a negative-lookahead on "merged", rather than exact multi-word phrases) or explicitly instruct workers/reviewers to check *all* locations a claim ID or its grounds could recur, not just the two known ones. This is the same double-occurrence trap the PM audit flagged for T-MIN-015, just recurring in a third place the audit didn't anticipate — worth a standing reviewer checklist item: "grep the claim ID across the whole file, not just the claims table and §3."
- **The `--reviewer`/`--model` flags on `start-review` regenerate a fresh template that discards any in-progress notes if re-run.** Not a bug encountered here, but worth flagging: if a reviewer's session is interrupted mid-review after `start-review` but before `record-review`, re-running `start-review` silently overwrites the template's `reviewed_head_sha` default and blanks findings back to `REQUIRED_PLEASE_FILL`. A `--resume` flag or a refusal to overwrite an already-edited template (mtime check) would avoid losing partial review work.
- Everything else about the reviewer workflow (`git worktree add` at the exact reviewed sha, running the audited verification command verbatim, `start-review`/`record-review`) worked cleanly and is well-suited to isolating review work from the shared repo checkout. No complaints on the happy path.
